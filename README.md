## Analysis #1: Asian Paints (Mar-24 to Mar-26)

**File:** [Asian Paints Analysis.xlsx](Asian%20Paints%20Analysis.xlsx)

### Summary

Asian Paints is a low-debt, profitable company. FY Mar-25 was weak: sales fell 4.48% and net profit fell 32.84%. Mar-26 shows recovery, but profit is still below Mar-24.

**Scope:** This is my first company analysis. It covers ratio analysis (profitability, returns, leverage and efficiency). Later analyses will add cash flow, peer comparison and DCF valuation.

### Key Numbers (₹ crore)

| Metric | Mar-24 | Mar-25 | Mar-26 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales | 35,494.73 | 33,905.62 | 35,583.54 |
| Operating Profit | 7,584.98 | 6,006.21 | 6,699.73 |
| Net Profit | 5,460.23 | 3,667.23 | 4,325.35 |

|---|---|---|---|

### How the New Ratios Were Calculated

| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| Number of Shares | Equity Share Capital / Face Value (Rs 1) = 95.92 Cr |
| EPS | Net Profit / Number of Shares |
| Book Value per Share | (Equity Share Capital + Reserves) / Number of Shares |
| P/E Ratio | Market Price / EPS |

### Key Findings

1. **Profitability:** Margins fell in Mar-25 and recovered in Mar-26, but have not reached Mar-24 levels.
2. **Debt:** Debt-to-equity is low, but borrowings rose from Rs 2,290 crore to Rs 3,929 crore in Mar-26.
3. **Debt safety:** Interest coverage is above 26x in every year, so profit easily pays the interest.
4. **Returns:** ROE fell from 29.15% to 18.90%, then recovered to 20.24%.
5. **EPS:** Shares stayed at 95.92 Cr, so EPS moved only with profit: Rs 56.92, Rs 38.23, Rs 45.09.
6. **Valuation:** P/E peaked at 61.22 in Mar-25. EPS fell about 33% but the price fell only about 18%, so P/E went up.
7. **Mar-26:** EPS recovered to Rs 45.09 but the price fell further to Rs 2,165.20, so P/E eased to 48.02.

# Company Analysis

Financial ratio analysis of Indian paint companies, built in Excel.
Data source: Screener.in. All figures in Rs crore, years ending March 2024 to March 2026.

## Files
- File: Berger Paints (2).xlsx

## What each workbook covers
- Profit and loss: sales, operating profit, net profit, interest
- Growth and margins: sales growth, net profit growth, operating margin, net profit margin
- Returns: ROE, ROA, EPS
- Efficiency: asset turnover
- Safety: interest coverage, debt to equity
- Valuation: book value per share, P/E ratio

## Berger Paints: key findings (Mar-24 to Mar-26)
- Sales grew 3.09% in Mar-25 and 2.91% in Mar-26
- Operating margin fell from 16.62% to 15.43%
- Net profit margin fell from 10.43% to 9.49%
- Net profit rose to 1,180.40 crore in Mar-25, then fell to 1,126.87 crore in Mar-26
- ROE fell from 21.71% to 16.29%, because equity grew 28.58% while net profit fell 3.5%
- Debt to equity fell from 0.14 to 0.09
- Interest coverage rose from 23.79 to 31.84 (based on operating profit)

Conclusion: Berger Paints is a low-debt company with strong interest coverage.
Sales are growing, but margins, ROE and asset efficiency are declining.
